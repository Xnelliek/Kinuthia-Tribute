Order of Program

  1. Processional Soft opening music as guests take their seats — Instrumental
  2. Welcome Remarks Opening words & purpose of the gathering — Service Leader / Host
  3. Thematic Lighting Dedication of the three memorial candles — Family & Loved Ones
  4. Main Lighting Passing the flame & lighting the central Master Candle — All Guests
  5. Musical Reflection Memorial hymn: "Abide With Me" — Musician / Vocalist
  6. Silent Moment One minute of quiet reflection & prayer — All Attendees
  7. Personal Tributes Shared memories from Mum, Mumbi, family & friends — Designated Speakers
  8. Closing Blessing Final words & invitation to the balloon release — Service Leader

Thematic Candle Readings

1st Candle · Love — lit by Mum, Njeri Ndekei

"We light this candle to represent love, warmth, and the everlasting bonds we share with family and friends. Your love as a son will remain in our hearts forever."

2nd Candle · Hope — lit by Mumbi Hinga

"We light this candle for hope, looking forward to the future with optimism and light, holding tightly to the precious moments and dreams we shared."

3rd Candle · Memory & Community — lit by Family & Friends

"We light this candle to honor those who came before us and the strong community that surrounds and supports us today in your memory."

Memorial Hymn · Abide With Me

Abide with me; fast falls the eventide;
The darkness deepens; Lord, with me abide.
When other helpers fail and comforts flee,
Help of the helpless, O abide with me.

I fear no foe, with Thee at hand to bless;
Ills have no weight, and tears no bitterness.
Where is death's sting? Where, grave, thy victory?
I triumph still, if Thou abide with me.
