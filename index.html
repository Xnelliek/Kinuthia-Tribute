Obituary & Life Story

“A beautiful soul lives on in our hearts forever.” Kinuthia Ndekei was born on January 29, 1998, in Parklands, Nairobi, bringing immense joy to his devoted parents, Peter Ndekei Kinuthia and Njeri Ndiho Ndekei. Raised in Lower Kabete, Kinuthia grew up as a vibrant and inquisitive child whose early years were shaped by a strong Christian foundation, built on love, faith, and family values.

Education

Kinuthia attended Consolata School in Westlands, Nairobi from baby class in 2001 to 2011 where he sat for his KCPE and initial secondary school before transitioning to Likii Hill School in Nanyuki, Laikipia where he sat for his KCSE in 2015. During his time in school, Kinuthia stood out as a dynamic and multi-talented student who truly shone in sports, earning well-deserved medals in swimming, football, and athletics. His natural capability and early independence were clear to all—highlighted by the astounding feat of learning to drive at the tender age of 13.

Career & Work Life

Shortly after completing high school, Kinuthia volunteered as an intern at Boxraft Limited (Kenyans.co.ke) before completing his International Computer Driving Licence (ICDL) at the Institute of Software Technologies in Nairobi. In 2016, he enrolled at KCA University (Ruaraka Campus) to pursue a Diploma in Investment and Financial Analysis, later earning his certified Lifeguard and Swimming Instructor accreditation from the YMCA in Nairobi in 2018.

Driven by a passion for technology, he undertook foundational and advanced web development training in Nairobi in 2020. In December 2021, Kinuthia relocated to the Seattle,US to advance his education, successfully graduating from Sumner College in 2025 and passing his board exams. Deeply committed to lifelong learning, he was eagerly preparing to continue his academic journey prior to his untimely demise.

Family Life & Character

Kinuthia was deeply cherished by his beloved parents, remaining a loving, obedient and hardworking son. Confident and articulate, he always expressed his thoughts with clarity and possessed a remarkable sense of discernment. A person of diverse interests, Kinuthia genuinely enjoyed music and reading books. He was the anchor of many treasured family memories, holding an irreplaceable place in the hearts of his mum and dad. Anchored by steady confidence, Kinuthia brought his girlfriend Mumbi Hinga into the warmth of his family’s fold, where she was embraced not just as a friend, but as a cherished daughter.

Faith & Final Journey

Upon confession of his faith in the Lord Jesus Christ as his personal Savior, Kinuthia was publicly baptized by immersion on December 3, 2006, at CITAM Parklands. On Saturday, September 5, 2026, Kinuthia peacefully rested in his sleep. May his soul rest in eternal peace.

“The Lord gave and the Lord has taken away; may the name of the Lord be praised.” - Job 1:21b
